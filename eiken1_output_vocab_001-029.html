② 英和一覧（全表現・例文つき）

例文 が付いているものが、①で出題された本書の主力表現。

英語を見て意味を言う練習用
表現意味例文
001～を求める
push for ~例文～を求める、～を強く要求するLocal people are pushing for stricter gun-control regulations.
demand～を要求するThe workers demanded higher wages.
seek～を求める、探し求めるShe is seeking a new job.
call for ~～を要求する、呼びかけるCritics are calling for his resignation.
002基盤、土台
bedrock例文根幹、揺るがぬ基盤Freedom of speech is the bedrock of democracy.
foundation土台、基礎Trust is the foundation of any relationship.
cornerstone礎、最も重要な要素Education is the cornerstone of economic growth.
basis基礎、根拠His theory forms the basis of modern physics.
003～を脅かす
pose a threat to ~例文～に脅威をもたらすCyberattacks pose a threat to national security.
endanger～を危険にさらすPollution endangers marine life.
imperil～を危険に陥れる（硬い語）The scandal imperiled his career.
threaten～を脅かすRising sea levels threaten coastal cities.
004（問題など）に対処する
address例文（問題）に取り組む、対処するWe have to address the problem urgently.
handle～をさばく、処理するShe handled the complaint calmly.
work on ~～に取り組むThe team is working on the bug.
deal with ~～を扱う、対処するWe must deal with climate change now.
005極めて重要な
crucial例文極めて重要な、決定的なTourism is crucial to the economy.
extremely important非常に重要なSleep is extremely important for students.
critical決定的に重要な、危機的なThe next few days are critical.
vital不可欠な、生命に関わるWater is vital to life.
006～に頼る
reach out to ~例文～に助けを求めて連絡するHe reached out to his friend for career advice.
consult～に相談するYou should consult a lawyer.
seek help from ~～に助けを求めるShe sought help from her teacher.
look to ~～を頼りにするMany young people look to their parents for support.
007警戒すべき
alarming例文警戒すべき、憂慮すべきIt's alarming that global warming is accelerating.
worrying心配なThe results are worrying.
disturbing動揺させる、不穏なHe told us a disturbing story.
troubling気がかりなThe report contains troubling data.
008豊富な
ample例文十分な、たっぷりあるThe country has ample natural resources.
bountiful豊かな（収穫・恵みに）They enjoyed a bountiful harvest.
plentiful豊富な、たくさんのFish are plentiful in this river.
abundant豊富な、ありあまるThe region has abundant rainfall.
009～を少し修正する
make a tweak to ~例文～を少し手直しする（口語)We made a few tweaks to the schedule.
slightly changeわずかに変えるHe slightly changed the wording.
slightly modifyわずかに修正するThey slightly modified the design.
slightly alterわずかに改変するShe slightly altered her plan.
010悪影響
detrimental effect例文有害な影響Chronic stress has a detrimental effect on our health.
adverse effect逆効果、副作用The drug may have adverse effects.
harmful effect害のある影響Smoking has harmful effects on the lungs.
negative effect否定的な影響Noise has a negative effect on concentration.
011違法な取引
unlawful trade例文違法な取引We must combat the unlawful trade of narcotic drugs.
illegal trade違法取引（最も一般的）The illegal trade in ivory continues.
illicit trade不正取引（定番の組み合わせ）Police cracked down on the illicit trade in weapons.
illegitimate trade正当性のない取引The company was accused of illegitimate trade.
012～に不安がある、～を心配する
be apprehensive about ~例文～に懸念を抱いているSome people are apprehensive about self-driving vehicles.
be worried about ~～を心配しているI'm worried about the exam.
be anxious about ~～を案じているShe is anxious about her future.
013改革を始める
institute reforms例文改革を実施するThe government will institute reforms in the health sector.
begin reforms改革を始めるThe city began reforms last year.
launch reforms改革に着手するThey launched reforms in education.
initiate reforms改革を開始するThe new leader initiated economic reforms.
014（差異・変化などが）著しく
markedly例文目立って、著しくThe company's sales increased markedly.
significantly相当に、有意にPrices have risen significantly.
noticeably目に見えてHis English has improved noticeably.
considerablyかなり、相当The population has grown considerably.
015主に
primarily例文主として、第一にThe land is used primarily for agricultural purposes.
mainly主にThe audience was mainly students.
mostly大部分はThe staff are mostly women.
chiefly主として（やや硬い）The city is known chiefly for its castle.
016法律を制定する
bring in legislation例文法律を導入するThe government brought in legislation to regulate pollution.
introduce legislation法案を提出するThey introduced legislation on data privacy.
establish legislation法律を確立するThe country established legislation against child labor.
017不十分な
insufficient例文（量が）足りないThere was insufficient rainfall for the crops to grow.
deficient（栄養などが）欠乏したHis diet is deficient in vitamins.
inadequate（質・水準が）不十分なThe safety measures were inadequate.
018欠点、不利な点
drawback例文難点、マイナス面The former model has some drawbacks.
downside悪い面The downside is the high cost.
disadvantage不利な点Living far from the station is a disadvantage.
shortcoming短所、不備The report points out several shortcomings.
019期待［予想］を上回る
exceed expectations例文期待を上回るThe final sales figures exceeded our initial expectations.
surpass expectations予想を凌ぐThe film surpassed all expectations.
top expectations予想を超えるProfits topped expectations this quarter.
020実際に役立つ
practical例文実践的な、現実的なHe took a practical approach to the problem.
down-to-earth地に足のついた（主に人柄）She is a down-to-earth person.
realistic現実的なWe need a realistic plan.
021（アイデアなど）を思い付く
conceive例文～を着想する（硬い）We conceived a brilliant idea.
come up with ~～を思い付く（最も自然）He came up with a good solution.
think up～を考え出すShe thought up a clever excuse.
hit upon ~ふと～を思い付くThey hit upon a new method.
022～に資金を割り当てる
appropriate funds for ~例文～に予算を計上する（動詞は -プリエイト）The city council appropriated funds for disaster relief.
allocate funds for ~～に資金を配分するThe school allocated funds for new computers.
allot funds for ~～に資金を割り当てるThey allotted funds for research.
023永久に
in perpetuity例文永久に（法律・契約の語）They wish to preserve the tradition in perpetuity.
forever永遠にI will remember this day forever.
eternally永久に、いつまでもHe is eternally grateful for her help.
permanently恒久的にThe factory was permanently closed.
024（解決のため積極的に）問題に取り組む
wrestle with an issue例文問題と格闘するThe school is wrestling with the serious issue.
grapple with an issue問題に取っ組み合うCities are grappling with the housing issue.
contend with an issue問題に対処する、渡り合うFarmers must contend with the issue of drought.
025～を危うくする
jeopardize例文～を危険にさらす、台無しにするOffice safety was jeopardized by the lack of first-aid kits.
put ~ in peril～を危険な状態に置くThe decision put the whole project in peril.
endanger～を危険にさらすReckless driving endangers lives.
026～を守る、～を保護する
safeguard例文～を保護する、擁護するNew measures were introduced to safeguard customer data.
secure～を安全に確保するThe army secured the border.
protect～を守るSunscreen protects your skin.
027苦しい戦い
uphill battle例文困難な闘い、骨の折れることObtaining permission was an uphill battle.
struggle苦闘、もがきLearning Japanese was a struggle for him.
difficult task難しい仕事Persuading them will be a difficult task.
028前進する
make headway例文（困難の中で）前進するWe are making headway in the negotiations for peace.
progress進展するThe project is progressing well.
advance進歩する、前進するTechnology advances rapidly.
move forward前に進むLet's move forward with the plan.
029迫りくる危機
looming crisis例文迫りくる危機There is a looming crisis in the pension scheme.
growing crisis深刻化する危機The country faces a growing crisis.
imminent crisis目前に迫った危機Experts warn of an imminent crisis.
impending crisis差し迫った危機They ignored the impending crisis.
